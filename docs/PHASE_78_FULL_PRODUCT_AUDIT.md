## Executive Gap Summary (Discovered Across Phases 78–95 Scope)

While the local-first business OS foundation is strong and cleanly separated, full product inspection surfaced **10 concrete remaining implementation gaps** between the verified Phase 73–77 checkpoint and the complete Phase 78–95 specification:

1. **Project & Task Voice Notes (Phase 82.1) — `MISSING`:** No `MediaRecorder` / `getUserMedia` capture utility, no audio validation/preview/pause/resume/playback UI on Projects or Tasks, no dedicated IndexedDB `voiceNotes` store separated from relational task/project rows, and no `/api/v1/voice-notes` endpoint.
2. **Project & Task Comments / Attachments / Members (Phase 82) — `PARTIAL`:** Projects/Tasks support subtasks, milestones, risks, OKRs, Gantt, Kanban, and dependencies, but lack collaborative comments, assignee/member attribution, and Vault attachment linking.
3. **Centralized Audit Log System (Phase 85) — `PARTIAL`:** Fragmented across basic `activities` (`ActivityView.tsx`), basic `securityAuditLogs` (`SecurityCenterView.tsx`), and minimal SQLite `audit_logs`. Missing typed 16-event taxonomy, correlation IDs, outcome (`success`/`failure`), resource type/ID attribution, secret/PII metadata scrubber, retention pruning, transactional persistence, paginated `/api/v1/audit-logs/:workspaceId` + `/export` API, and a full centralized read-only Audit Log screen with actor/date/action/resource/outcome filters, detail modal, and export.
4. **CRM Lead Stages, Contacts & Linked Communication (Phase 81) — `PARTIAL`:** Clients support CRM CRUD, duplicate checks, tags, notes, CSV import/export, and project/invoice counts, but lack explicit CRM lead pipeline stages (`new` -> `won`/`lost`), secondary contacts, linked contracts/appointments summary, and direct follow-up scheduling actions.
5. **Document Versioning & Explicit Approval vs E-Signature Disclosure (Phase 83) — `PARTIAL`:** Proposals & Contracts export PDFs cleanly, but lack structured revision history tracking, attachment linking, and consistent internal-approval vs legal e-signature differentiation in UI.
6. **Server-Side Financial Calculation Verification (Phase 84) — `PARTIAL`:** Frontend uses `Big.js` decimal precision (`calculateDocumentTotals`), but backend API lacks a server-side invoice total calculation/verification endpoint (`POST /api/v1/invoices/calculate`).
7. **BI Data Lineage & AI Business Query Presets (Phases 86–87) — `PARTIAL`:** AI Gateway & 15-Agent Workforce exist cleanly, and BI reports calculate aging/forecasts/estimates vs actuals, but Reports/Dashboard should explicitly tag `observed_data` vs `heuristic_projection` with dataset lineage, and Copilot needs explicit Invoice Explanation & Document Summarization workflows.
8. **Workflow Automation Multi-Trigger Depth & Recursion Guards (Phase 88) — `PARTIAL`:** Engine covers proposal & overdue invoice approvals cleanly, while Custom Workflows (`customWorkflows` store) lack runtime rule evaluation with depth/loop recursion guards and per-workspace execution rate caps.
9. **Centralized Integration Hub & Communication Controls (Phases 89–90) — `PARTIAL`:** Integrations (webhooks, payment connectors, calendar/email, AI providers) are scattered across 4 screens without a unified Hub (`IntegrationHubView`), backend `/api/v1/integrations/:workspaceId` registry (secret-free responses), disconnect/revoke, health overview, or message template dispatch confirmation + duplicate send prevention in `CommunicationHubView`.
10. **SaaS Billing Session/Webhook Enforcement, Setup Checklist, Privacy Account Wipe & Expanded E2E (Phases 91–95) — `PARTIAL`:** Billing catalog & Pakistan payment guidance exist, but lack `POST /api/v1/billing/checkout-session` (honest blocked state), `POST /api/v1/billing/webhook/:provider` (HMAC + idempotency), server-side entitlement guard, team member invite/role UI in Settings/Cloud, dismissible onboarding setup checklist, privacy consent & deletion controls, and expanded Playwright E2E coverage.
