# Business Reporting, Cash Flow & Monthly Goals Guide

## Weekly & Monthly Review Cadence
- **Cash Flow View**: Track actual cash collected (`Income`) minus cash spent (`Expenses`) per month alongside open receivables (`Invoices Balance Due`).
- **Invoice Aging**: Separate receivables into Current, 1–30 days, 31–60 days, and 61+ days past due so collection outreach prioritizes highest-risk cash gaps first.
- **Monthly Goals**: Compare actual monthly revenue, billable hours, and expense ceilings against pre-set targets in Dashboard Business Health.

## Multi-Currency Discipline
Never add raw numbers across mismatched currencies without converting or grouping per currency. Keep client invoices, payments, and quotation math aligned in each engagement's agreed currency.
