# Time Tracking & Estimates vs Actuals Guide

## Why Track Time Even on Fixed-Price Projects
Fixed-price contracts still depend on hourly yield (`Net Profit / Total Hours`). Tracking hours reveals which service packages generate strong effective rates and which cause hidden margin erosion.

## Billable vs Non-Billable Split
- **Billable Time**: Direct discovery workshops, design, coding, writing, testing, and client sprint reviews.
- **Non-Billable Time**: Cold outreach, proposal drafting, invoicing administration, and internal tooling maintenance.
- Healthy solo utilization typically ranges from **60% to 75% billable** of total working hours.

## Preventing Estimation Drift
1. Store estimated hours per service catalog item and per project budget (`Budget / Hourly Rate`).
2. Compare estimated hours vs logged actual hours weekly in Reporting.
3. Adjust future quotation pricing upward when consecutive projects exceed hour estimates by > 20%.
