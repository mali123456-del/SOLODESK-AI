# Client Communication & Follow-Up Playbook

## Communication Principles
- Be clear, concise, and concrete about dates, deliverables, and next actions.
- Separate **verified workspace facts** (invoice numbers, due dates, logged hours, completed milestones) from **recommendations or estimates**.
- End every client message with one explicit next step and owner.

## Core Message Frameworks
1. **Discovery Call Recap**: Summarize client goal, agreed deliverables, open questions, and exact proposal delivery date.
2. **Weekly Sprint Update**: List (a) Completed this week, (b) Scheduled next week, (c) Decisions or assets needed from the client.
3. **Friendly Payment Reminder (1–14 Days Overdue)**: Reference invoice number, amount due, due date, and payment instructions politely without accusatory phrasing.
4. **Scope Change Confirmation**: Acknowledge request enthusiastically, clarify impact on budget/timeline, and attach a short quotation option before starting extra work.
