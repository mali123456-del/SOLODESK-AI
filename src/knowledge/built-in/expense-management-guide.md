# Expense Management & Categorization SOP

## Standard Freelance Expense Categories
- **Software & Subscriptions**: SaaS tools, design licenses, IDE subscriptions, productivity suites.
- **Hosting & Cloud**: AWS, Vercel, Cloudflare, domains, DNS, staging servers.
- **Hardware & Equipment**: Laptops, monitors, keyboards, audio gear, office chairs.
- **Subcontractors & Collaborators**: External QA, illustration, copyediting, specialist dev support.
- **Marketing & Outreach**: Portfolio hosting, sponsorships, SEO tools, promotional assets.
- **Bank & Payment Fees**: International wire deductions, Wise/Payoneer FX fees, card processing fees.
- **Office & Utilities**: Coworking passes, business internet, dedicated phone plans.
- **Travel & Meetings**: Client site travel, transit, lodging.

## Important Informational Disclaimer
SOLODESK expense logs and monthly summaries are **informational estimates for freelancer operations**, not certified accounting, audited tax filings, or statutory financial advice. Preserve original vendor receipts outside the browser and review year-end totals with a qualified accountant.
