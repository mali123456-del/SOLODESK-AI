# Freelancer Business Basics — Solo Operations Playbook

## Core Operating Loop
Independent consultants and small studios stay profitable when five operational pillars stay connected:
1. **Lead Qualification**: Confirm budget range, decision timeline, problem urgency, and stakeholder authority before writing a custom proposal.
2. **Scope & Pricing Discipline**: Quote deliverables, assumptions, revision limits, and payment milestones explicitly. Avoid open-ended fixed bids without change-order rules.
3. **Time & Budget Visibility**: Track both billable and non-billable hours (admin, sales, QA) so effective hourly yield reflects true effort.
4. **Milestone Invoicing**: Collect an upfront kickoff deposit (typically 30%–50% on fixed scopes) and invoice immediately when milestones complete.
5. **Cash Reserve & Informational Bookkeeping**: Maintain a separate tax/runway reserve and record every business expense cleanly for tax preparation review by a certified accountant.

## Standard Freelance Engagement Checklist
- Verify client billing entity, contact email, and preferred currency before kickoff.
- Attach signed proposal or contract acceptance notes before creating sprint tasks.
- Track all work against a project budget so scope creep is caught early.
- Export regular offline JSON backups of workspace data.
