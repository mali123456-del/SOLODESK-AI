# Project Profitability & Effective Hourly Rate Guide

## Key Metrics for Freelance Projects
- **Recorded Income**: Verified invoice payments and direct income entries linked to the project.
- **Direct Project Expenses**: Subcontractors, cloud hosting, stock assets, or licenses tied to delivery.
- **Estimated Net Profit**: `Recorded Income - Direct Project Expenses` (computed with exact decimal math).
- **Effective Hourly Rate**: `Estimated Net Profit / Total Logged Hours`.

## Evaluating Project Health
1. **High Margin / High Hourly Yield**: Ideal candidate for packaging into your Service Catalog as a repeatable fixed-scope tier.
2. **High Revenue / Low Effective Hourly Rate**: Signals underestimated scope, excessive unbilled revisions, or heavy support overhead. Raise price or cap revisions next engagement.
3. **Unbilled Tracked Value**: Compare `Billable Hours * Rate` against issued invoices monthly to ensure no completed time goes unbilled.
