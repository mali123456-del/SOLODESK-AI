# Client Management & Lead Pipeline Guide

## Client Lifecycle Stages
- **Lead**: Prospective client exploring scope, budget, or availability. Needs structured discovery notes and next follow-up date.
- **Active**: Client with an accepted proposal, active project, or recurring retainer.
- **Archived**: Completed or dormant relationship preserved for historical invoices, tax records, and reactivation outreach.

## Lead Qualification Framework (BANT-Lite for Solo Operators)
Before investing hours in bespoke proposals, document four essentials in the client record:
1. **Problem Statement**: What business metric or workflow bottleneck is broken today?
2. **Budget Alignment**: Does their expected investment match your minimum engagement or service catalog tier?
3. **Decision Timeline**: When must delivery finish, and who gives final sign-off?
4. **Success Criteria**: How will both parties verify the deliverable is complete?

## Duplicate Prevention & CRM Hygiene
- Match existing clients on normalized lowercase email and exact contact name before creating duplicates.
- Segment clients using clear categories (E-Commerce, SaaS, Consulting, Design) and operational tags (Retainer, Priority, Referral).
- Log meeting notes immediately after discovery calls and flag follow-up items so no lead cools off unaddressed.
